Why applications actually get rejected

A vague profile paragraph

Reviewers skim for a concrete domain. "Final-year CS student, writes Python and C++ daily, spends most of my time debugging other people's code" beats "hardworking and detail-oriented" every time.

Short answers on the sample task

The assessment is the interview. Almost everyone who fails wrote too little. Explain your reasoning, not just your answer — the reasoning is the product these companies sell.

A country mismatch found at payout

You can sign up from anywhere. You cannot get paid from anywhere. Confirm eligibility before you invest two hours in an assessment.

Applying to everything in one afternoon

Several of these platforms use the same fraud-detection vendors. A burst of near-identical applications in one day reads as farming. Four this week, spaced a couple of days apart.

Giving up during the silence

Two to three weeks of no reply is normal, not a rejection. The people who earn are the ones still checking the dashboard in week four.

Claiming skills you can't show

Most platforms gate each skill behind its own test, and a failed test can lock that category for months. Claim what you can prove, then add more later.
